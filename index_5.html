Trip Info

🧳 Packing (Singapore → UK December)

  • 3-layer system beats one thick coat: wicking base + fleece/light down mid + waterproof shell.
  • Waterproof jacket, NOT umbrella — lakeside wind destroys umbrellas.
  • Waterproof walking boots with grip — the #1 purchase. Paths are muddy/icy and never gritted.
  • Hat, gloves, scarf — damp cold bites extremities first.
  • Torch (cabin farm track + Castlerigg), UK 3-pin adapters, meds.

🌦 December reality (Met Office averages)

  • London 9°/3°C · Manchester 8°/1°C · Peak District 6°/1°C · Pitlochry ~7°/1°C · Lakes 8°/2°C.
  • Rain days: 11–18/month by stop. Expect grey + drizzle; 2–4 crisp clear days if lucky.
  • Sunset ~15:45 everywhere. Big lakes do NOT freeze (Ullswater last froze 1963, Loch Lomond ~1880). Ground ice on clear mornings — walk after 10am.

🚗 Driving

  • Left side, same as Singapore. Automatic must be reserved explicitly. No winter tyres needed (UK norm).
  • Met Office warnings: Yellow = drive normally · Amber = delay/buffer the rural leg · Red = don't travel.
  • A82, A66, M-ways gritted. Cabin track: no low-slung cars. Native + NCP car parks: 2.10m height limit.
  • Rental: full-to-full fuel, photos at pickup AND return, licence + passport + driver's credit card.

💷 Money

  • No tourist VAT refund since Jan 2021 — outlet/shop prices are final. Contactless works everywhere, incl. buses/trams/tube.
  • Congestion charge: Marylebone Rd is boundary (free); weekends charged only from 12:00. Rental cars are ULEZ-compliant.
